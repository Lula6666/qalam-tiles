Qalam Tiles قلم

Upcycled tiles that each carry one Arabic word in calligraphy. Set them on a paper folio to compose a proverb, then hang the folio on the frame, where everyone can see it and hear it read aloud.

3The hanging frame

Finished folios hang on the cords. Tap one to hear it read aloud. Several visitors can fill folios at the same time, and the frame fills up together.

Mockup for discussion. The frame borrows from the Muʿallaqāt, “the suspended” pre-Islamic odes. A later legend says they were written in gold and hung on the Kaaba; most scholars doubt this, and the name may mean “the precious ones” (Britannica). The odes were mostly recited, so every folio can be heard. The frame is a market stall, recalling the poetry fair at Ukaz, not a sacred site. Tag reads and audio are simulated here.
