The craft table and the wall frame قلم

A six-sided craft table seats six, each with their own kit tray, so nobody waits in line. Visitors choose a proverb, make one of its words as a tile, scan it, finish the proverb with tiles from neighbors and the word library, hang it on the wall frame, and take home a card with their name. Together

Six seats, each with a full kit tray. Shared supplies sit on a turntable in the middle.

What happens

Each word sticker carries a small QR code. The seat screen shows the word, and any phone can play it aloud.

What’s on the table

At each of the six seats

A kit tray with a word sticker sheet, a tracing card, a pencil, and a glue stick. Next to it: three proverb cards, a small seat screen, and a folio.

On the turntable in the middle

Shared bins of blanks in four shapes (already faced with paper), packs of pre-cut edge strips in color and gold, glue, pencils, and the word library: tiles made earlier, reused all day.

Around the table

The example board on the easel shows how a tile is made and the finished shapes. The carved wall frame holds up to 50 folios. The gift printer makes name cards.
