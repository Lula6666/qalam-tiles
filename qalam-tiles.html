Qalam Tiles قلم

Upcycled cardboard tiles that carry one Arabic word as calligraphic image. The edge holds transliteration and translation, and an exposed NFC tag links to sound and meaning. Arrange tiles on the rack, right to left, to compose a proverb.

Concept prototype for the “Representational and Substrate Roles in Paper Co-Centered Tangibles” manuscript. Tag reads are simulated here; on a physical tile, an NTAG sticker sealed face-up under Mod Podge can be read by most phones. Script styles shown are Kufic, Naskh, and Ruqʿa.
